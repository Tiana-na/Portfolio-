Background

UX/UI Designer · Digital Creative Designer

Instead of store & customer journey, a screen & user flow.

After thorough professional training in digital design and UX/UI, I now bring the knowledge and experience I built over years in commercial design and customer experience into the world of digital products. I’ve learned to work with UX processes, research, specification, and interface design, translating both user and business needs into simple, effective digital solutions.

I believe good design connects aesthetics, usability, and purpose,
and that the experience should always be clear and intuitive.
